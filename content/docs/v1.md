Version 1 will make the compact parser the only engine. The project will graduate every grammar
the legacy engine supports, then delete the legacy engine. This includes all 206 default grammars
and the opt-in Lean 4 grammar. A known difference needs proof that the grammar cannot graduate
and the owner's approval.

The [v1 design](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/blob/main/docs/v1-design.md) is the
current plan. The [roadmap](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md)
points to it for decisions, gates, and status.

## Where it stands

As of 2026-09-27, production GLR parsing is the default. Compact parsing is opt-in with
GTS_ADMISSION_CANDIDATE=1, a setting added in
[PR #1264](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/pull/1264). The language graduation
allowlist is empty, so no language has graduated to compact by default.

The milestones track the work. Issue [#1293](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1293)
for M0 is closed. Issues [#1294](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1294),
[#1295](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1295),
[#1296](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1296), and
[#1297](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1297) for M1–M4 are open.

## How one language graduates

A language joins the compact allowlist only with a receipt for that language. The design requires:

- No unexplained fresh-tree differences against the C oracle.
- Incremental results match fresh Go parses through the 72-step edit session, with 16 tested sites
  per edit class.
- Reuse matches or exceeds the legacy route; after scanner certification, clean steps must reuse.
- No unexplained corpus declines. Each allowed decline needs an artifact-specific deny entry and a
  condition for reopening it.
- Tokens, new nodes, and maximum live versions stay at or below legacy on every fixture.
- The 20-seed median time stays at or below legacy at 32 KiB, 137 KiB, and 1 MiB.
- Peak resident memory at 1 MiB stays at or below legacy.
- Crash, memory-budget, out-of-memory, and race checks pass in the dedicated container.
- Highlighting and tags keep parity, including supertype and MISSING patterns.

The design groups languages by graduation cohorts: 1a is Go; 1b is C#, Elixir, HTML, Markdown,
PHP, and Python; cohort 2 is the rest of the top 20; cohort 3 is the rest of the top 50; cohort 4
covers the remaining supported grammars, including Lean 4. The
[design's cohort table](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/blob/main/docs/v1-design.md#graduation-cohorts)
is the source for membership.

## v1.0 speed targets

The v1 design's D13 targets are:

| Layer | Scope | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Engine floor | All 206 grammars | Median at most 3x C; no file above 5x; no cliff failures. |
| Tuned | Top 50 grammars | Median at most 2.5x C; no language above 4x; 99th-percentile edit latency at most 20 ms at 137 KiB. |
| Stretch | Top 20 grammars | Full parse at most 2x C; edit latency at or below C. |

Owner decision O-Q3 makes the engine-floor and tuned targets release-blocking for v1.0. Stretch
targets remain directional.

## v1.0 release gates

The design requires the project to:

- Graduate all top-50 languages and delete the legacy engine.
- Graduate every other grammar supported by legacy, including opt-in Lean 4. A known difference
  requires proof that graduation is infeasible, a witness, the blocking mechanism, and owner
  approval.
- Meet the engine-floor and tuned speed targets above.
- Require subtree reuse for every top-50 language and keep 99th-percentile edit latency at or
  below 20 ms at 137 KiB.
- Match C's recovery result on at least 60 of 79 cases and always match C's HasError result.
- Publish the v1 API, versioning policy, grammar format v1, and a dashboard for all 206 grammars.
- Pass tests on all six D-D3 platforms: Linux amd64 and arm64, macOS arm64, Windows amd64 and
  arm64, and wasip1.

## Milestones

These are planned weeks, not calendar dates. Current issue status was checked on 2026-09-27.

| Milestone | Planned weeks | Issue | Focus |
|---|---:|---|---|
| M0 Reset | 0–2 | [#1293](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1293), closed | Establish repeatable baselines, correctness and counter gates. |
| M1 Slim core | 2–8 | [#1294](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1294), open | Reduce the runtime core, add build and platform gates, and run the Q0 experiment. |
| M2 Cohort 1 | 8–16 | [#1295](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1295), open | Graduate cohort 1 and remove its cliffs and invariant mismatches. |
| M3 Top 50 | 16–26 | [#1296](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1296), open | Graduate cohorts 2 and 3 and complete top-50 deprecations. |
| M4 One engine | 26–32 | [#1297](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues/1297), open | Graduate the remaining grammars, delete legacy, and flip the global default in v1.0.0-rc.1. |

The Q0 experiment runs for two weeks, from 2026-09-26 through 2026-10-10.

## What to do today

You do not need to change an application. The production route remains the default. To try compact
parsing in a process, set GTS_ADMISSION_CANDIDATE=1 and compare its trees and performance on your
own inputs.

Follow the
[milestone issues](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Av1-milestone)
and the upstream
[v1 design](https://github.com/odvcencio/gotreesitter/blob/main/docs/v1-design.md). A useful
contribution is a small failing input with its grammar name and expected tree, or a reproducible
performance case attached to the relevant issue.
